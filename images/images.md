# Image Generation Prompts for City Care Hospital

This document contains high-fidelity, realistic photography prompts for all 20 image assets referenced across the **City Care Hospital** project.

> **Generation Guidelines for AI Image Generators (Midjourney v6 / DALL-E 3 / Flux / Imagen 3):**
> - **Style**: Editorial architectural & medical documentary photography.
> - **Camera / Lens**: Shot on Canon EOS R5 / Sony A7R V with 35mm f/1.8 or 85mm f/1.4 lenses, natural clinical lighting.
> - **Avoid**: Cartoons, 3D renders, CGI plastic skin, fake exaggerated smiles, or cluttered fantasy medical equipment.
> - **Aspect Ratios**: 
>   - Hero: `16:9` or `21:9`
>   - Facilities & Rooms: `16:10` or `4:3`
>   - Doctors (Headshots): `1:1` (Square)
>   - Testimonials (Portraits): `1:1` (Square)
