Preventive Wellness

Daily Health Tips from Our Physicians

Prevention is always better than cure. Simple daily habits significantly reduce cardiovascular and lifestyle diseases.

Hydration & Kidney Health

Drink 2.5 to 3 liters of clean water daily to assist renal filtration and maintain cardiovascular balance.

Daily 30-Minute Brisk Walk

Moderate aerobic movement lowers bad LDL cholesterol, elevates HDL, and regulates fasting blood glucose levels.

Low Sodium & High Fiber Nutrition

Reduce packaged snacks and table salt to under 5g per day to prevent hypertension and arterial stiffness.
