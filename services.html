Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding hospital admission, insurance desk, visiting hours, and emergency protocols.

Emergency patients are brought directly to our 24/7 Casualty Wing at Gate 1. Initial triage, resuscitation, and pain management begin immediately without demanding upfront paperwork or financial clearance. Family members can complete registration paperwork once the patient is stabilized.

Yes. We are empaneled with all prominent Third-Party Administrators (TPAs) and health insurers including Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Max Bupa, Care Health, and government employee health schemes. Our dedicated TPA desk on the Ground Floor operates from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM to facilitate pre-authorization.

To ensure strict infection control and undisturbed patient recovery: General and private ward visiting hours are 05:00 PM to 07:00 PM daily. In Intensive Care Units (ICUs), only one immediate relative is permitted between 05:30 PM and 06:30 PM with sanitized gowns and protective shoe covers.

Yes, our NABL-accredited diagnostic laboratory offers phlebotomist visits at home anywhere in the city. You can schedule sample pickup by calling our diagnostic helpline at +91 141 270 8888. Verified digital reports are delivered via email and WhatsApp within 4 to 8 hours.

You can view and cancel any active consultation directly through our website on the Appointment Page under the "My Scheduled Appointments" manager, or by calling our OPD desk at least two hours prior to your scheduled time.
